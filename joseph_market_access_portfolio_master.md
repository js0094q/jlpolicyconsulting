This master file links the portfolio deliverables and keeps the narrative aligned around one theme: Joseph Stewart translates reimbursement mechanics into commercially usable strategy for complex provider-administered products across launch, in-market, and mature lifecycle stages.

## Core Story
- Clinical value alone does not determine uptake.
- Provider reimbursement architecture changes adoption behavior.
- 340B is a launch-economics issue, not just a compliance issue.
- GTN leakage has to be decomposed before it can be recovered.
- The best strategy is reimbursement-aware, site-specific, and operationally executable.
- A head of market access cares about breadth: launch, on-market management, provider economics, formulary, 340B, and field tools all have to line up.
- The operating model has to be useful to leadership, not only to reimbursement specialists.
