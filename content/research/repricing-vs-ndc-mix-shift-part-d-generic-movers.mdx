Raw quarter-over-quarter mover tables are useful for screening, but they can overstate true repricing when composition changes are not controlled.

## Audit Framework

For `2025Q4` to `2026Q1`, the audit applied:

1. Persistent same-NDC matched medians.
2. Days-supply stratification.
3. Driver labeling across categories: `true_repricing`, `ndc_mix_shift`, `duplicate_mapping_issue`, `thin_sample`, and `mixed`.

## Driver Mix Across Family-Level Movers

At family level (`n=57`), driver labels were:

- `22` mixed
- `12` true repricing
- `9` duplicate mapping issues
- `7` NDC mix-shift dominant
- `7` thin-sample signals

This distribution indicates that only a subset of large raw moves should be interpreted as clean repricing.

## Examples: Signal Survives vs Signal Weakens

### Cases where raw movement weakens after same-NDC control

- rivaroxaban: raw `-34.1%` vs matched `+0.02%`.
- tolvaptan: raw `-10.5%` vs matched `-0.6%`.

### Cases where repricing remains clear after control

- dapagliflozin: raw and matched both `-68.2%`.
- meperidine hydrochloride: raw and matched both `+290.1%`.
- hydrocortisone: raw and matched both `+401.6%`.

Across family rows with both raw and matched values (`n=36`), `5` rows showed raw moves of at least `20%` that shrank to `5%` or less after controls.

## Market Significance Filter

Confirmed repricing should still be filtered by footprint:

- triamcinolone acetonide affected `4,382` plans but only `0.203%` of total generic pricing rows.
- dapagliflozin showed large matched repricing but only `41` affected plans and `0.000519%` row share.

Large percentages without broad footprint can mis-prioritize action.

## Caveats and Confidence Labels

- `thin_sample` and `duplicate_mapping_issue` rows are hypothesis-generating, not decision-grade.
- Authorized-generic carve-outs should be separated in core generic narratives.
- This is a quarter-pair snapshot and does not establish causal intent.
