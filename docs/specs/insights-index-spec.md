# Insights Index Spec
## JL Policy Consulting Website

## Purpose

The Insights index is the editorial listing page for shorter-form policy and reimbursement commentary.

Its job is to:
- present published insights clearly,
- support scanning and discovery,
- reinforce the site’s authority,
- encourage deeper reading.

This page should feel editorial and selective, not promotional.

---

## Primary Audience

Primary audiences include:
- readers looking for current policy and reimbursement commentary,
- prospective clients evaluating the quality of thought,
- industry stakeholders browsing specific topics.

---

## Primary User Questions to Answer

1. What kinds of topics does this site publish on?
2. Are the insights specific, serious, and useful?
3. How can I quickly find relevant items?

---

## Primary Conversion Goal

Primary CTA:
- `Read Insight`

The page should primarily drive article reading.

---

## Secondary Conversion Goals

Secondary CTAs may include:
- `Explore Research`
- `View Consulting`
- `Contact Us`

Use sparingly.

---

## Required Tone

The Insights index must feel:
- editorial,
- high-quality,
- analytical,
- easy to scan,
- restrained.

It must not feel:
- cluttered,
- content-farmed,
- oversized,
- overly promotional.

---

## Required Page Structure

Use this structure:

1. Header
2. Insights Intro
3. Insights Grid / List
4. Optional Topic / Category Navigation
5. Optional Featured Insight
6. Optional CTA Band
7. Footer

---

## Section Requirements

### 1. Insights Intro
Must include:
- concise page title,
- short description of what “Insights” means on this site.

This should frame the section as shorter-form policy, reimbursement, and market access commentary.

### 2. Insights Grid / List
This is the core of the page.

Each card should include:
- title,
- summary,
- publish date,
- category,
- optional tags,
- clear read action.

Cards should be easy to scan and visually consistent.

### 3. Optional Topic / Category Navigation
If implemented, category or tag filtering should be clean and restrained.

Do not overcomplicate the interface.

### 4. Optional Featured Insight
Use only if it improves the page.

A featured item may be used to highlight an especially strong or timely piece, but should not overpower the index.

### 5. Optional CTA Band
A light CTA may direct readers to:
- Research
- Consulting
- Contact

Keep it subtle.

- over-design article previews,
- crowd the page with too much metadata.

Prefer:
- strong titles,
- clear summaries,
- easy scanning,
- clean card rhythm,
- restrained metadata hierarchy.

---

## Design Rules

The Insights index should:
- feel lighter and more editorial than the Consulting page,
- preserve the site-wide typography and spacing rules,
- use clean listing patterns,
- avoid oversized previews or heavy homepage-style modules.

---

## Success Criteria

The Insights index is successful if:
- the listing is easy to scan,
- the editorial quality feels high,
- visitors can quickly identify relevant insight pieces,
- the page reinforces authority without clutter.
