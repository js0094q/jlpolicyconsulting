Part B negotiation timeline, and why it matters for this role

For initial price applicability year 2028, CMS finalized the third-cycle negotiation framework on September 30, 2025, published the selected-drug list on January 27, 2026, required participation agreements by February 28, 2026, required manufacturer and public submissions by March 1, 2026, scheduled public engagement for April 2026, must send initial offers by June 1, 2026, gives manufacturers until July 1, 2026 to accept or counter, allows additional negotiation through summer 2026, requires CMS final offers by September 30, 2026 if needed, ends the negotiation period on November 1, 2026, publishes agreed MFPs by November 30, 2026, and makes those prices effective January 1, 2028.  

For this role, that timeline matters because Part B negotiation is not just a pricing-policy event. It changes operational reimbursement. CMS says Medicare pays most separately payable Part B drugs at ASP plus 6%, and in the CY 2026 PFS final rule CMS clarified that units sold at the MFP are included in ASP calculations effective January 1, 2026. CMS also stated that for quarters in which Medicare payment is based on MFP, the Medicare Part B Drug Payment Limit File will display the MFP-based payment limit. In other words, once a Part B AbbVie product is selected, the issue is not only “what is the negotiated price,” but also “how does that price flow through ASP reporting, payment-limit files, provider reimbursement, and gross-to-net expectations.”
