2. Drug billing layer

For Adrabetadex itself, the key launch issue is whether there is a product-specific HCPCS code yet.

Early launch, before clean product-specific coding

In HOPD, newly approved drugs/biologicals without a specific HCPCS pathway often begin under temporary or miscellaneous billing logic such as:

* C9399
* J3490
* J3590

CMS materials and MAC articles show these are commonly used interim pathways for newly approved drugs or biologics before a permanent/product-specific code is in place. Payment then relies on OPPS/NOC pricing files or contractor processes.  

More mature post-launch

Once Adrabetadex receives a product-specific HCPCS code and enters the quarterly pricing file process, payment becomes cleaner and more standardized. CMS pays most separately payable Part B drugs using ASP-based methodology, with quarterly pricing file updates.  

3. Administration procedure layer

For the intrathecal administration, the strongest current analog remains:

* CPT 96450 for administration into the central nervous system requiring spinal puncture

That is not Adrabetadex-specific, but it is the clearest current Medicare analog for an intrathecal drug given via lumbar puncture. CMS MAC guidance for Spinraza specifically expects 96450 on the same date of service.  

4. Professional physician work layer

Physician time is generally billed in only two ways:

* through the procedure code’s RVUs for the intrathecal administration
* through a separate E/M code only if the physician performs a significant, separately identifiable evaluation beyond the work inherent in the procedure

CMS is explicit that same-day E/M requires separation from the usual pre-, intra-, and post-procedure work, typically with modifier 25 when appropriate.  

Relevant professional E/M families, when justified, are typically:

* 99202–99205 for new outpatient visits
* 99211–99215 for established outpatient visits

5. Monitoring and diagnostic layer

Separate medically necessary monitoring may generate separate claims, but under their own code families, not by “adding time” to the administration claim.

Examples:

* Audiology services are separately covered and paid under PFS or OPPS depending on setting. CMS confirms audiology is covered as a diagnostic test and paid under the Physician Fee Schedule or OPPS as appropriate.  
* Genetic testing, if covered and medically necessary, is billed under the relevant laboratory/genetic test pathway
* A separate physician reassessment visit may be billed via E/M if it is distinct and medically necessary

6. Facility layer in HOPD

Because you have identified hospital outpatient as the site of care, the facility economics matter more than people often think.

The hospital may also bill facility-side services such as:

* clinic assessment/management, when requirements are met, typically G0463
* facility drug administration and support costs through OPPS logic
* packaged operational burden that may not be separately recognized line-by-line

CMS states hospitals may report drug administration services and facility-based E/M such as G0463 when significant and separately identifiable.  

7. OPPS/pass-through layer

For Adrabetadex, this is likely one of the most important payment levers.

CMS states that transitional pass-through payments are available for certain new drugs and biologicals under OPPS, and the application process runs through MEARIS. CMS’s current MEARIS site lists deadlines and confirms drug and biological pass-through applications are submitted there.
